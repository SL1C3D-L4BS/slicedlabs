Builder brief:
workspace::create(path, options) and workspace::open(path, open_mode, recovery_policy) on log_backend + vfs_posix;
  open takes the OFD writer lock (writer_locked; a lock file not held by a live process is taken over and reported),
  runs recovery_steps R1-R10 of SL-ARCH-002, freezes registries and returns last_recovery(). Read-only open needs no
  lock.
checkpoint.hpp: writer and reader for the SLCK format (checksum over all bytes); invalid -> ignored and reported
  (checkpoint_invalid finding).
recovery.hpp: recovery_report {lost_bytes, truncated_frames, orphan_objects, open_intents, heads, used_checkpoint,
  quarantined, findings}; torn tail bytes are copied to quarantine/ before truncation; mid-log damage and chain breaks
  are findings handed to WO-059 policies (default here: refuse write open with journal_corrupt). commit_indeterminate:
  the manager refuses commits with recovery_required until reopen; reopen decides by frame intactness; a keyed retry
  returns the receipt.
testing/crash_harness: seeded workloads against the model oracle; crash after primitive k for every k via faulty_vfs
  (policies drop_unsynced, keep_unsynced, tear_last_append).
Recipes: fabric-crash-sweep runs `ctest --preset debug -R "^fabric_recovery_.*sweep"`; fabric-soak iters runs the
  crash sweep with random seeds (seed printed on failure), never part of check; neg_fabric_crash_sweep.sh edits a copy
  so the head is published before the objects are durable (the script asserts the edit) and requires the sweep to fail
  naming FAB-INV-RCV-09.
Tests: integration recovery_truncation_sweep_test FAB-INV-RCV-01, recovery_bitflip_sweep_test RCV-02,
  recovery_crash_sweep_test RCV-09, recovery_optrace_test RCV-11; unit recovery_chain_test RCV-03,
