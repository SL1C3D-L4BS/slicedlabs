# WO-082 deviations: the job system (episode 086)

## Background results are jobs::background_result, not det::external_input

- Decision: `drain_results` delivers `jobs::background_result {job_seq, post_index, kind, bytes}`; frame and tick
  stamping is left to the frame loop.
- Why: replay.hpp (`det::external_input`) arrives with WO-084 in wave 4; jobs.hpp cannot include it yet. WO-084 or
  WO-089 maps a background_result to an external_input and stamps frame and tick.
- Where: engine/runtime/include/sl/runtime/jobs.hpp `background_result`, `job_system::drain_results`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R030.

## Results are held back behind earlier background jobs

- Decision: the results of a completed background job are delivered only after every background job submitted before
  it has completed; within a job, in post order.
- Why: "in submission order" (ARCH3-R030) with out-of-order completion needs a hold-back; ordering by submit sequence
  keeps replay deterministic.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::drain_results`, `background_done`, `register_background`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R030.

## current_job_priority is a member found by thread id, not a thread_local

- Decision: `job_system::current_job_priority() const` looks up the calling thread's slot by `std::thread::id` and
  returns its running job's priority, nullopt outside a job or on a foreign thread.
- Why: R-RT-GLOBAL refuses `thread_local`, and tools/runtime/lint_allow.json is outside paths_allowed. Open issue: an
  allowlisted thread_local would make the lookup O(1).
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::current_job_priority`, `find_slot`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R030, R-RT-GLOBAL.

## fatal_report and on_fatal

- Decision: `jobs::fatal_report {code, job, name, worker}` is defined in jobs.hpp; `job_config::on_fatal` is called on
  the worker after the body returns and before the job finishes (dependents are cancelled after the sink returns).
- Why: SL-ARCH-003 names a "fault sink" without a type; the runtime diagnostics sink is a later work order.
- Where: engine/runtime/include/sl/runtime/jobs.hpp `fatal_report`, `job_config::on_fatal`;
  engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::report_fatal`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R067, `failpoints` RFP3.

## RFP2, RFP3 and RFP4 visit sites

- Decision: RFP2 fails the job with job_failed before its body, RFP3 fails it fatally (job_failed through on_fatal);
  RFP4 stalls min(param, 2000) us at before_pop, after_steal, before_complete and in a wait.
- Why: the `failpoints` table names the points but not the sites; the 2000 us cap keeps unit cases under 1 s.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `fault_before_body`, `stall`.
- Rule or table: `failpoints` RFP2, RFP3, RFP4.

## Background floor in the scheduler, not the reference policy

- Decision: every `aging_limit`-th scheduling round a worker polls the background queue first (also when
  `promote_background` says so). The owner thread runs background jobs only with workers = 0 or while stopping.
- Why: the reference policy has no aging (policy_simple.cpp, brief), yet a background job must complete under a
  saturating frame stream (RT-INV-JOB-08).
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::take`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R030, `limits` aging_limit.

## Shared queues are locked_deque; only local queues come from make_queue

- Decision: the frame injection, background and main-affinity queues are `locked_deque` built by the job system;
  `job_config::make_queue` builds only the per-worker local queues.
- Why: the shared queues are multi-producer; a work-stealing deque (WO-086) is single-owner.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::start`, `make_shared_queue`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R070, `limits` injection_queue_capacity.

## Wakeups: notify_all on every push and completion

- Decision: every ready push and every completion calls `event_count::notify_all`.
- Why: simplest lost-wakeup-free rule for the reference policy; WO-086 may narrow it to notify_one.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `make_ready`, `finish`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R064.

## Counter and fence dependencies are polled gates

- Decision: a job with a pending job_counter or cpu_fence dependency is held in a gate list polled (try_lock, at most
  16 per poll) by taking threads; while gates are pending threads yield instead of parking.
- Why: job_counter and cpu_fence (WO-080) have no completion callback to hook.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::poll_gates`, `help_until`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R062.

## Only workers and the owner help in wait

- Decision: a thread that is neither a worker nor the owner parks in `wait` instead of running jobs.
- Why: it has no slot or local queue; helping from it could run main-affinity or background work on a foreign thread.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::wait`, `help_until`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R064.

## Inline runs at capacity return the null handle

- Decision: a submit at capacity with no ready job and no predecessor runs the job inline and returns
  `job_handle{}`; its failure is not observable through wait (status of the null handle is stale_job, wait is ok).
- Why: no record was allocated, so there is no generation to name.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::submit_impl`, `run_inline`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R065.

## A stale predecessor counts as satisfied

- Decision: a dependency on a handle whose generation no longer matches (or the null handle) is satisfied at submit.
- Why: the record was reused, so its outcome is lost; the job it named has finished.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system_records.cpp `job_system::attach`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R063.

## submit() errors return the null handle

- Decision: `submit` (the helping variant) returns `job_handle{}` on any error and counts it in `job_stats::rejected`;
  `try_submit` returns the error.
- Why: submit is the infallible convenience call of the brief; the counter keeps refusals visible.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::submit`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R065.

## Record pool and cold side array

- Decision: records are freed into a FIFO ring; the 128-byte record holds the brief's hot fields and four fixed
  dependent links; `done`, `seq`, the error code, the cancel flag and the overflow links live in a parallel
  `record_cold` array.
- Why: FIFO reuse delays generation reuse; the 128-byte limit leaves no room for the cold fields.
- Where: engine/runtime/include/sl/runtime/jobs.hpp `record`, `record_cold`; engine/runtime/src/job_system_records.cpp.
- Rule or table: `limits` job_record_bytes, job_max_dependencies.

## Name and scope lengths

- Decision: a job name and a correlation scope are stored as pointer plus 16-bit length (capped at 65535); both views
  must outlive the job.
- Why: the record has no room for owned strings; names are static by contract and scopes are literals.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::submit_impl`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R068.

## Thread names and instance id

- Decision: worker threads are `slrt<platform instance>-w<k>` and the watchdog `slrt<instance>-wd` (the platform
  thread_factory names them); `job_config::instance_id` appears only in the watchdog report.
- Why: thread naming belongs to the platform layer (WO-078).
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::start`; job_system_watchdog.cpp `start_watchdog`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R069.

## Config ranges

- Decision: `workers` defaults to `default_workers()` and must be <= 64 (0 runs every job on the owner); `capacity`
  must be 1 to 2^24; anything else is invalid_argument from start.
- Why: `limits` gives the default and max_workers; 2^24 keeps an index times 8 dependency slots in 32 bits.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::start`.
- Rule or table: `limits` workers_default, max_workers, job_capacity_default.

## job_fn checks by static_assert

- Decision: the templated `job_fn` constructor rejects a callable that is not nothrow-invocable, too large (48 B),
  over-aligned (16) or not nothrow-movable with static_assert, so `std::is_constructible` stays true for such types.
- Why: a static_assert names the failed rule; a constraint would only say "no matching constructor".
- Where: engine/runtime/include/sl/runtime/jobs.hpp `job_fn::job_fn`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R061, `limits` job_inline_payload_bytes.

## Cancelled jobs are routed to the frame or local queue

- Decision: a job cancelled by a failed predecessor is queued on the frame (or the releasing worker's local) queue
  regardless of affinity or priority; its body never runs, only completion bookkeeping.
- Why: a cancelled main-affinity job would otherwise wait for the owner to drain it.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::make_ready`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R066, ARCH3-R067.

## stop and the destructor

- Decision: `stop` cancels every job that has not started, waits for running ones and joins the threads; a second
  `stop` is ok; the destructor shuts down when stop was not called. The owner-only void calls (run_main_affinity,
  drain_results, quiesce) check the thread with SL_DEBUG_ASSERT.
- Why: the brief gives wrong_thread only for stop (result<void>); the void calls have no error channel.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp `job_system::stop`, `shutdown`, `~job_system`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R071, `thread_safety`.

## Tests: no integration tier; the planted lost wakeup is a hiding queue

- Decision: all eight invariants are unit tests (tests/unit/runtime/job_*_test.cpp); no tests/integration file was
  added. RT-INV-JOB-07 plants the lost wakeup with a test work_queue that hides pushed jobs and uses the steady clock.
- Why: every case fits the unit budget; the job system has no hook to drop a wakeup on purpose.
- Where: tests/unit/runtime/job_watchdog_test.cpp `hiding_queue`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R069, RT-INV-JOB-07.

## neg-runtime-race strips the marker lines of a copy

- Decision: the gate deletes every line containing `// neg-race-lock` (the four lock lines and the header comment that
  names the marker) in a copy and builds it with a planted driver, after a clean control run of the unmodified copy.
- Why: the control run proves the driver itself is race-free, so the report comes from the missing lock.
- Where: tests/gates/neg_runtime_race.sh.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R070.

## DAG test group counters are sized once

- Decision: the RT-INV-JOB-01 DAG test holds its per-group job_counters in a std::vector sized (n / 97) + 1 up front
  and addresses them by index; job bodies keep a pointer to their group's counter.
- Why: cppcheck (`just analyze`) reported invalidContainer for a pointer into the growing std::deque; a container
  that never grows makes the pointer's lifetime evident without a suppression.
- Where: tests/unit/runtime/job_dag_test.cpp `run_dag`.
- Rule or table: RT-INV-JOB-01.

## NOLINT for bounded indices and the nothrow ring

- Decision: `cppcoreguidelines-pro-bounds-constant-array-index` is suppressed (NOLINTBEGIN/END with the bound) in
  `attach`, `release_dependents` and `poll_gates` and on the gate append; `avoid-c-arrays` on the
  `unique_ptr<std::uint32_t[]>` rings; `bugprone-suspicious-stringview-data-usage` on the name pointer.
- Why: the indices are checked against fixed bounds; a std::vector ring would throw on allocation failure.
- Where: engine/runtime/src/job_system.cpp, job_system_records.cpp, queue_locked.cpp.
- Rule or table: not covered.

## make_ws_queue after WO-086 (WO-106)

- Decision: the RT-INV-JOB-01 case that asserted make_ws_queue returns unsupported now asserts make_ws_queue(16)
  succeeds with capacity() 16 and that job_system::start succeeds with it; the reference-policy checks stay. The
  "unsupported until WO-086" comments in work_queue.hpp are replaced.
- Why: WO-086 replaced the stub with ws_deque; the old assertion called error() on a value and aborted.
- Where: tests/unit/runtime/job_dag_test.cpp; engine/runtime/include/sl/runtime/work_queue.hpp `make_ws_queue`.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R070, ARCH3-R073.
