## S1 runs 20 DAG iterations over 8 seeds on the ws queue

- Decision: the S1 DAG fuzz runs 20 iterations (scaled), cycling 8 seeds derived from the cell seed, on the job
  system's default queue (ws_deque) only. The locked queue is covered by S2.
- Why: the 20 s Debug cap per file (stress S1 and S3 share stress_jobs_test.cpp).
- Where: tests/integration/runtime/stress_jobs_test.cpp.
- Rule or table: `stress_matrix` S1, ARCH3-R162.

## S4 checks the first and last byte of each block

- Decision: S4 allocates from one tracking_resource per thread plus a shared one (every 16th block) and writes and
  checks only the first and last byte of each block, not every byte.
- Why: a full fill and compare of blocks up to 64 KiB dominates the cap; the first and last byte still catch an
  overlapping or short block. The per-tag balance and the leak report are checked in full.
- Where: tests/integration/runtime/stress_alloc_test.cpp `churn`.
- Rule or table: `stress_matrix` S4, ARCH3-R162.

## S5 sweeps a 2000-operation batch on one world with sampled hashes

- Decision: S5 arms RFP0 at every allocation of a seeded 2000-operation structural batch (748 allocations in the dry
  run), not of 2000 allocations, on one world: for each operation RFP0 is armed at its 1st, 2nd, ... allocation until
  the operation succeeds, and every out_of_memory leaves the world version and the oracle unchanged. The world hash is
  compared around the first failure of every step-th allocating operation (step 1 at scale 1).
- Why: 2000 operations make fewer than 2000 allocations, so "every index of 2000 allocations" is not reachable;
  replaying the batch per index took 30 s, and a SHA-256 world hash per failure took 32 s in Debug.
- Where: tests/integration/runtime/stress_alloc_test.cpp, S5 test case.
- Rule or table: `stress_matrix` S5, `failpoints` RFP0, ARCH3-R162.

## S6 pre-warms the frame arenas; the 1e5 row is short and summary-only

- Decision: each S6 cell warms both frame-arena sets of every worker before the first frame (the RT-INV-FRAME-04
  pattern); "zero upstream allocations" counts the frame arena's upstream and the rig's tracking upstream after
  warmup_frames_default (10) frames. The 1e3 and 1e5 entity rows are scaled. The 1e5 row runs 5 ticks on workers
  {1, 16} and compares a per-tick summary (population, energy, alive, archetypes) instead of fixture_hash, and makes
  no steady-frame claim.
- Why: without the warm-up, the first use of a worker's arena after frame 10 allocates upstream and logs the
  steady-frame WARN; the fixture hash costs about 3 s per tick at 1e5 entities in Debug, and the 1e5 population
  transient grows arena demand past any warm-up.
- Where: tests/integration/runtime/stress_ecs_test.cpp `warm_arenas`, `run_cell`, `check_row`.
- Rule or table: `stress_matrix` S6, ARCH3-R166.

## S7 runs 20 ticks per cell, not 300

- Decision: every cell of the determinism matrix (10 policy and worker cells plus 8 RFP4 chaos cells) runs 20 ticks
  (scaled) of sim_fixture at 4400 entities.
- Why: a Debug tick with its fixture hash costs about 37 ms; 18 cells x 300 ticks would take about 200 s against the
  20 s cap. runtime-soak repeats the matrix under fresh seeds.
- Where: tests/integration/runtime/stress_det_matrix_test.cpp.
- Rule or table: `stress_matrix` S7, ARCH3-R084.

## The harness watchdog's own spawn visits RFP1

- Decision: run_watched spawns its body on the platform's os_thread_factory, which visits RFP1; tests arm RFP1 only
  inside the watched body (after that spawn).
- Why: the watchdog needs a real thread, and the lint bans std::thread in engine/runtime/testing.
- Where: engine/runtime/testing/stress_harness.cpp `run_watched`; stress_lifecycle_test.cpp.
- Rule or table: `failpoints` RFP1, ARCH3-R020.

## Checks run on the test thread

- Decision: watched bodies record outcomes into plain structs; every doctest CHECK runs on the test thread after the
  body returns.
- Why: doctest's INFO context (the seed rerun line) is thread-local.
- Where: every tests/integration/runtime/stress_*_test.cpp.
- Rule or table: ARCH3-R165.

## RFP0 is armed at the memory root or around world operations only

- Decision: STRESS-03 and STRESS-08 arm RFP0 on a tracking_resource or an entity allocator or around a structural
  world operation, never across a whole runtime frame.
- Why: follows WO-096 (an RFP0 inside playback faults the frame, which S8 covers through RFP5).
- Where: stress_alloc_test.cpp, stress_failpoints_test.cpp.
- Rule or table: `failpoints` RFP0.

## RFP8 is tested before the write and the crash sweep is the vfs's

- Decision: STRESS-08 checks RFP8 as implemented (a simulated crash before atomic_write: io_error, no primitive, the
  old file loads after a crash) and adds a faulty_vfs crash after every primitive of the new save (old or new loads).
- Why: the RFP8 site fires once before the write; the per-primitive crash is the faulty vfs's (as in RT-INV-SNAP-04).
- Where: tests/integration/runtime/stress_failpoints_test.cpp, RFP8 case.
- Rule or table: `failpoints` RFP8, ARCH3-R147.

## stress-matrix.json cells and seeds

- Decision: stress_report.py writes one cell per S row for the preset; cells from one file share its ctest wall time;
  the seed is the rerun line under the failing test case, else SL_RUNTIME_SOAK_SEED, else the cell's fixed seed (S2
  reports the locked_deque case's seed). `--merge` keeps other presets' cells; runtime-stress writes the debug cells
  only (the sanitizer recipes do not call the report).
- Why: the schema has one seed and one wall time per cell; the brief does not say how sanitizer cells get in.
- Where: tools/runtime/stress_report.py, scripts/runtime/runtime-stress.sh.
- Rule or table: schemas/runtime/stress-matrix.schema.json.
